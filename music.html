Brian B Greene · 4 September 2026

Tape Matter Memory

'Tape Matter Memory' is a collection of fifteen pieces developed through an engagement with cassette tape loops, voice, drums, stringed instruments, field recordings and synthesis. The pieces grew out of repeated gestures, close listening and small alterations. A loop stretches or wears; a sound comes around again without ever arriving in quite the same shape. Those shifts become part of the music, as familiar textures open into different rhythms and relationships. The collection traces a process of returning to an idea, letting it change, and discovering what materials can suggest. Written, recorded, produced and mastered by Brian B Greene. Artwork by Brian B Greene.

Kinnego Flux · 19 July 2024, Rudimentary Records

Satsuma

'Satsuma' is the eight-track album by Kinnego Flux, the collaboration between Brian Greene and David Baxter (aka Kab Driver). Its grooves bring together funk, electronic production, bass, drums, horns, synths and vocals. Early CD-R copies circulated around 2009 before the album received its official release in 2024. Written, produced, mixed and performed by David Baxter and Brian Greene; additional production and writing on 'Unravel' by Defcon. Mastered by Macc at Subvert Mastering. Artwork by David Baxter and Andrew McHugh; photo by Tina Moore.

Brian B Greene · 20 September 2019, Reset Industries

Echo & Xplode

'Echo & Xplode' is a four-track EP released by Belfast label Reset Industries. Recorded at Funkhaus Berlin, it is built around vocals, keys, bass and drums, moving between layered harmonies, intimate grooves and heavier electronic textures. 'Xplode' draws on an obscured memory of a bombing in Northern Ireland. All composition, performance, recording and mixing by Brian Greene.

Barry Lynn & Brian Greene · 9 March 2015, Kinnego Records

Remote Relations

'Remote Relations' is a three-track collaboration by Barry Lynn and Brian Greene, following earlier work together on Boxcutter's 'The Dissolve'. The EP places lyrical narratives and vocal arrangements over bass-led electronic productions, moving from the hollow swing of 'As If U' to the long-distance reflections of the title track and the wintry, spectral boogie of 'What It Means'. All tracks written and produced by Barry Lynn and Brian Greene. Artwork by Niadzi Muzira. Mastered by Bob Macc at Subvert Central Mastering.

Kab Driver · 3 September 2012, Airflex Labs

Kab Driver

Brian Greene contributed to three tracks on this album by Kab Driver: Batteria, Hare's Gap and Interlude. Batteria brings his falsetto vocals into a dubstep and funk collaboration with David Baxter and Barry Lynn. The album was released by Airflex Labs in 2012.

Boxcutter · 25 April 2011, Planet Mu

The Dissolve

Brian Greene contributed lyrics, vocals, vocal production and arrangement to three tracks on Boxcutter's album: 'All Too Heavy', 'The Dissolve' and 'Ufonik'. The title track was included in Bleep's 100 Tracks of 2011. Across the three pieces, hazy layered vocals sit within down-tempo funk, spacious electronics and dense synth textures. The tracks were co-arranged by Barry Lynn and Brian Greene.

Boxcutter & Kinnego Flux · 9 February 2009, Kinnego Records

A Familiar Sound

This single by Boxcutter and Kinnego Flux combines funk and soul with electronic production, hardware synthesis and falsetto vocals. It was premiered in a Scuba Electronic Explorations mix. Lyrics, vocals and vocal production by Brian Greene. Co-arranged by David Baxter, Barry Lynn and Brian Greene.
