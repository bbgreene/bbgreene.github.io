Brian B Greene: Tape Matter Memory
(September 4, 2026)

'Tape Matter Memory' is a collection of fifteen pieces developed through an engagement with cassette tape loops, voice, drums, stringed instruments, field recordings and synthesis. The pieces grew out of repeated gestures, close listening and small alterations. A loop stretches or wears; a sound comes around again without ever arriving in quite the same shape. Those shifts become part of the music, as familiar textures open into different rhythms and relationships. The collection traces a process of returning to an idea, letting it change, and discovering what materials can suggest. Written, recorded, produced and mastered by Brian B Greene. Artwork by Brian B Greene.

Kinnego Flux: Satsuma
(July 19, 2024, Rudimentary Records)

'Satsuma' is the eight-track album by Kinnego Flux, the collaboration between Brian Greene and David Baxter (aka Kab Driver). Its grooves bring together funk, electronic production, bass, drums, horns, synths and vocals. Early CD-R copies circulated around 2009 before the album received its official release in 2024. Written, produced, mixed and performed by David Baxter and Brian Greene; additional production and writing on 'Unravel' by Defcon. Mastered by Macc at Subvert Mastering. Artwork by David Baxter and Andrew McHugh; photo by Tina Moore.

Brian B Greene: Echo & Xplode
(September 20, 2019, Reset Industries)

'Echo & Xplode'EP is the fifth release on Belfast-based boutique label, Reset Industries. Recorded in the Funkhaus, Berlin, these four tracks maintain focus on vocals while deploying ambient keys, deep bass textures, intimate grooves and flowing arrangements. Densely layered vocal harmonies are at the heart of the soulful 'All is Real' and the beat driven meander of 'Half in Half'. Strong dynamic arcs weave their way through the productions of 'Carve a Blue Stone' and'Xplode', a song that captures the obscured memory of a bombing in Northern Ireland. All musical performances, compositions, recordings and mixing by Brian Greene.

Barry Lynn & Brian Greene: Remote Relations
(March 9, 2015, Kinnego Records)

Having collaborated on several Boxcutter albums, especially on The Dissolve (released on Planet Mu in 2011) these 3 pieces offer more emphasis on lyrical narratives and arrangements. Lead track "As If U" flips some D50 flutes, bass guitar and a jazz fusion sample into a deep, hollow-sounding track that is contrasted with lyrics exploring themes of benevolence and exploitation against a kind soul. "Remote Relations" (with multiply plays on BBC 6 Music) is a pulsing bass guitar driven track with a wistful vocal delivery musing on long distance relationships. The lyrics on "What It Means" lament on winter. A spectral boogie track full of instrumental overtones and crackling drums. All lyrics, vocal performances, vocal mixes by Brian Greene. Tracks co-arranged by Barry Lynn & Brian Greene.

Boxcutter: The Dissolve
(April 25, 2011, Planet Mu)

A collaboration on 3 tracks for this full length album by Boxcutter. One of the collaborations, the title track "The Dissolve", made Bleep: 100 tracks of 2011. Each piece utilises hazy and effect laden vocals. "All Too Heavy" is a down-tempo, funk driven piece with cosmic lyrics and choral refrains. "The Dissolve" is open and dreamy. "Ufonik" sparkles with intense synth hits, slow moving rhythms and dense vocal harmonies. All lyrics, vocal performances, vocal mixes of previously mentioned tracks by Brian Greene. Previously mentioned tracks co-arranged by Barry Lynn & Brian Greene.

Boxcutter & Kinnego Flux: A Familiar Sound
(February 9, 2009, Kinnego Records)

A collaboration on this single. "Familiar Sound" combines funk and soul with electronics using instruments, vocals and hardware synths. It was premiered on a Scuba's Electronic Explorations mix. It fuses RnB with a dark dubstep sensibility through rolling stabs of sound and falsetto vocals. All lyrics, vocal performances, vocal mixes by Brian Greene. Track co-arranged by David Baxter, Barry Lynn & Brian Greene.
