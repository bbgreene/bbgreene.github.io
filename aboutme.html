Brian Greene

Composer, Musician, Educator

I am from Northern Ireland and based in Berlin. I work with voice, electronics, drums, tape and other instruments, making solo releases and collaborating with artists across electronic and experimental music.

My teaching has focused on music production, sound engineering, and creative process. I have worked as a lecturer and programme coordinator in Northern Ireland and Berlin, developing courses and helping others build their own creative audio practice.

I studied Music Technology at Queen's University Belfast and completed postgraduate studies in further education at Ulster University, and an MA in Creative Music Production at Catalyst in Berlin.
